Privacy policy

Watches Calendar is a personal app used only by its owner.

  • It reads and writes calendar events through the Google Calendar API, only for the signed-in account, and only to show and edit them on the owner's Apple Watch.
  • Events are cached on the Watch itself. The sign-in token is kept in the Watch's Keychain.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's, and nothing is shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Signing out, or removing access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, stops all access.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
